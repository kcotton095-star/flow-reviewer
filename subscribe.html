Common Questions

Will this show up on the same charge as my bookkeeping fee?
No. The apps are completely separate subscriptions billed independently by Stripe. They will appear as distinct charges on your card — never bundled with your bookkeeping invoice.
Do I have to subscribe to both?
Not at all. You can subscribe to just one app, or both. Each is month-to-month and you cancel either independently at any time with no penalty.
How does Flow Receipt Capture get receipts into my Dropbox?
During onboarding you'll connect your Dropbox account once. After that, every receipt you snap is processed by AI and placed in your Flow Bookkeeping folder automatically.
Can I cancel if I change my mind?
Yes — cancel any app subscription any time from your Stripe billing portal. You'll retain access through the end of the current billing period. No cancellation fees, ever.
Is my payment information secure?
Checkout is handled entirely by Stripe, the same payment infrastructure used by companies like Amazon and Shopify. We never see or store your card number.
